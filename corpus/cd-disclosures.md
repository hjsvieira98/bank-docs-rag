> Fictional sample. Northline Bank is not a real institution. This disclosure was written for a software demo and has no legal effect.

## Terms and deposit

Northline offers certificates of deposit with terms of 6, 12, 24, and 60 months.

The minimum opening deposit for a certificate of deposit is $1,000.

Interest on a certificate of deposit is compounded daily and credited monthly.

## Early withdrawal

For a certificate of deposit with a term under 12 months, the early withdrawal penalty is 90 days of simple interest.

The early withdrawal penalty for a 24-month certificate of deposit is 180 days of simple interest.

## Grace period

The grace period after certificate of deposit maturity is 10 calendar days.

A withdrawal during the grace period has no early withdrawal penalty.
