> Fictional sample. Northline Bank is not a real institution. These clauses were written for a software demo and have no legal effect.

## Rate lock

A mortgage rate lock holds the interest rate for 45 calendar days from the date Northline issues the lock confirmation.

If it expires before closing, the interest rate resets to the posted rate.

A one-time rate lock extension of 15 days costs 0.125% of the loan amount.

## Late payments

A mortgage payment is late after 15 calendar days, and the late fee is 4% of the overdue monthly payment.

## Prepayment

Fixed-rate mortgage loans have no prepayment penalty.

An adjustable-rate mortgage has no prepayment penalty after the third year.

## Escrow

Borrowers pay one twelfth of estimated annual property taxes and insurance into an escrow account each month.

Northline may require an escrow cushion of up to two months.

## Adjustable rate

The introductory rate on a 5/1 adjustable-rate mortgage lasts 5 years, and later changes are annual.

On an adjustable-rate mortgage, the periodic cap is 2 percentage points and the lifetime cap is 5 percentage points above the initial rate.

## Assumption

The loan is not assumable by a buyer unless the law requires it.
