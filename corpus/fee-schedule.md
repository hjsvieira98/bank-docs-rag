> Fictional sample. Northline Bank is not a real institution. This schedule was written for a software demo and has no legal effect.

## Wire transfers

This consumer fee schedule is effective March 1, 2026.

An outgoing domestic wire costs $25.

An outgoing international wire costs $45.

An incoming international wire costs $15.

## Stop payment

A stop payment request remains in effect for 6 months and costs $30.

## Statements and checks

A paper statement costs $3 per month.

A cashier's check costs $10.

## ATM

An out-of-network ATM withdrawal costs $2.50.

Northline ATM withdrawals have no Northline fee.
