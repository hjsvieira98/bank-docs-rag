> Fictional sample. Northline Bank is not a real institution. These conditions were written for a software demo and have no legal effect.

## Opening deposit

The minimum opening deposit for an Everyday Checking account is $25.

## Monthly fee

The Everyday Checking monthly fee is $12.

The Everyday Checking monthly fee of $12 is waived when the average daily balance is at least $1,500, or when a qualifying direct deposit of at least $500 posts during the statement cycle.

## Overdraft transfer

An overdraft protection transfer from a linked savings account costs $10 per transfer.

## Funds availability

Cash and electronic deposits are available the same business day.

A check deposited with a teller is available on the next business day up to $500, and the rest is available on the second business day.

## Joint accounts

Each owner of a joint Everyday Checking account may withdraw the full available balance.
